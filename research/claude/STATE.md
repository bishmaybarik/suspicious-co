## What this increment did

1. Audited the raw file and established the three nested units of observation
   (source row → scraped entity → registered outward investment → Indian
   parent). Documented in `DATA_AUDIT.md`.
2. Reconstructed the corporate hierarchy as a directed forest and built five
   analytical datasets: unique entities, parent-child edges, complete
   root-to-entity ownership paths, parent-jurisdiction aggregates and
   parent-level aggregates.
3. Ran the exploratory analysis and recorded **17 candidate findings**
   (C-F001 to C-F017) in `FINDINGS.md`, each with denominator, robustness,
   mundane explanation and a falsification test.
4. Produced 41 tables and 8 figures, all regenerated by one command.
5. Recorded next directions in `IDEAS.md`.

## Key structural facts established

- 3,742 rows → **1,834 unique entities** → **186 UINs** → **28 parents**,
  across **122 jurisdictions**, depth 0 to 12.
- The hierarchy is a **forest**: acyclic, maximum in-degree 1, verified by
  assertion in `02_build_hierarchy.py`. 1,854 edges.
- Each UIN has exactly one level-0 entity; the 184 level-0 entities carry
  1,500 entities beneath them.
- Entities do recur across ownership paths, but rarely: 11 normalised names
  under two parent buckets (22 targets, 1.2%), split between genuine
  public-sector consortium vehicles and Reliance attribution artefacts.
- 20 named intermediaries were never scraped; they head subtrees holding 150
  entities. Their subtrees are re-anchored and flagged.
- The UIN string is structured and decodable (office / investment type /
  year / serial), validated three ways.

## Decisions taken, with reasons

| Decision | Reason |
|---|---|
| Entity = `target_id`, not row | all 14 structural variables are constant within `target_id`; the collapse is lossless |
| Graph namespaced by parent bucket, not by UIN | maximises connectivity: 95.1% of non-level-0 entities match a parent within their bucket versus 90.4% within their UIN |
| `level` is the primary depth measure | it is the dictionary-documented variable; `depth_graph` is carried alongside and every depth result is checked against it |
| Orphan subtrees re-anchored under the Indian parent with an implied-level offset | keeps 150 entities inside the path analysis rather than dropping them; flagged with `is_orphan_bridged` and `path_has_unobserved` |
| `stake == 0` treated as missing, not as a zero share | 406 entities, monotonically declining with depth, impossible as a real share |
| Financial comparisons only within a currency | `currency` missing on 63% of rows, `units` on 98% |
| Findings kept descriptive | protocol requirement; nothing here supports inference about conduct |

## Known limitations carried into increment 2

1. Measured depth may partly reflect disclosure practice. The parent-level
   correlation between depth and source-location rate is only 0.065, which
   argues against the disclosure story, but the two extreme cases (Reliance,
   Hindalco) are still consistent with it and need external validation.
2. The vintage analysis (C-F008) rests on 184 level-0 entities in bins of
   16–54. It is suggestive, not estimated.
3. The financial layer is thin: 444 entities with a parsed balance sheet,
   265 valuation-ready, mixed currencies, missing units.
4. Entity counts are inflated by non-controlling holdings; C-F015 gives the
   control-weighted denominators that should be used going forward.
5. The UIN decoding is an inference from three consistency checks, not
   documented metadata.
